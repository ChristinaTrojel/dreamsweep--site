Privacy Policy

Effective: June 1, 2026

DreamSweep™ is developed by Christina Trojel-Hansen. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and your rights.

Data We Access

With your explicit permission, DreamSweep reads from Apple Health:

  • Sleep analysis (sleep stages, bed/wake times)
  • Heart rate samples
  • Heart rate variability
  • Date of birth (for age-adjusted scoring)

How Data Is Used

All data is processed entirely on your device. The App does not transmit, upload, or share your health data with any server, third party, or analytics service.

Data Storage

Scores are stored locally. Deleting the App removes all data. The App does not write to Apple Health.

Third-Party Services

DreamSweep does not integrate analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Your Rights

Revoke access anytime in Settings > Privacy > Health > DreamSweep. Delete all data by uninstalling.

Children

Not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Updates will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
